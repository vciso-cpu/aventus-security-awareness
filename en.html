Security Awareness
Dear colleague, congratulations, you were simulated for phishing

You have just taken part in a planned phishing simulation conducted as part of the information security awareness program.

The purpose of this simulation is education and prevention, not control or punishment.

What happened

During the simulation:

  • You received an email imitating a corporate message from SupportIT
  • The email contained a link to a look‑alike internal password change resource
  • You followed the link and entered data on the page
⚠️ This behavior is typical for real phishing attacks and can lead to account compromise in case of a real threat.
Important to know
  • 🔒 The data you entered was not saved or used
  • ❌ Passwords were not checked or shared with third parties
  • 📊 Only the fact of interaction was recorded (visit / form submission)
Phishing signs to watch for

In this scenario, typical phishing signs were used:

  • domains similar to the real ones
  • a sense of urgency (“within 48 hours”)
  • a request to enter credentials via a link
  • redirecting to an external site
  • visual similarity to internal services

Even if an email looks credible, always verify the sender and the link address.

Going forward

We recommend:

  • 🔍 carefully checking the sender address
  • 🌐 hovering over links before clicking
  • 🔐 never entering passwords from email links
  • 🚩 using the “Report phishing” function in the email client
